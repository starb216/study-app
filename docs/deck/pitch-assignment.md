## Jean — Integration, Dashboard, Core Functions, Admin, Aesthetics, Streaks

**Slides to cover:**
- Slide 2: The problem
- Slide 3: The person
- Slide 4: The market
- Slide 5: The competition
- Slide 6 (Demo): Notes, Shared Resources, Friends, Admin, Streaks, plus transition between demo cards
- Slide 8: What works
- Slide 9: The price
- Slide 10: The ask

### Talking points

**Slide 2 — The Problem**
- State the one-sentence problem: students juggle too many apps.
- List the apps: planner, calendar, timer, notes, sleep tracker.
- Key line: “Nothing talks to each other.”

**Slide 3 — The Person**
- Define the user: high school and university students.
- What they want: one place to plan, focus, rest, and compete with friends.
- Read the quote as if it came from a real user interview.

**Slide 4 — The Market**
- Say the market is huge but fragmented.
- Use the stat: 6+ apps switched per study session.
- Conclusion: a single study-first workspace wins.

**Slide 5 — The Competition**
- Contrast Notion/Calendar and Forest/Pomodoro.
- Why they fall short: too generic or too narrow.
- Why StudyMint wins: plan, focus, rest, notes, and shared resources in one place.

**Slide 6 — Demo (Your Cards)**
- Introduce the demo slide as the team’s feature showcase.
- For **Notes**: explain import/export, AI review sheets, flashcards, and quiz generation.
- For **Shared Resources / Courses**: explain course-based material sharing (e.g., Integrated Maths 2, Precalc).
- For **Friends + Admin + Streaks**: briefly mention social accountability, admin panel, and streak system as glue that keeps users coming back.
- Transition between each teammate’s card so the demo feels like one coherent walkthrough.

**Slide 8 — What Works**
- Share 3–4 concrete learnings from building and testing.
- Public ranks drive competition.
- Friends + leaderboard drive daily return.
- Nature themes make focus feel calmer.
- Notes + shared resources keep everything in one workspace.
- User surveys and real feedback are driving last-minute improvements.

**Slide 9 — The Price**
- State plainly: free for every student.
- Mention future premium ideas only if asked (AI review sheets, analytics, class leaderboards).
- Keep this slide under 20 seconds.

**Slide 10 — The Ask**
- Three clear asks: try it, give feedback, vote for StudyMint.
- Read the URL one last time.
- End with a direct competition ask: “If you want students to have one focused workspace, vote for StudyMint.”
- Thank the audience.
