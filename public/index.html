      How to use this calendar

      1. Pick a date

      Click any day in the calendar. The right panel updates to that date. Past days show a diagonal FINISHED tag; today has a black border.

      2. Add the most important thing

      Type something like 8:30-9:30 meeting under "What is the most important thing?". The calendar day changes shape and color based on keywords:

      KeywordsShapeColor
      homework, exam, class, study, meeting, assignment, project, quiz, school…HexagonLight blue
      family, birthday, mom, dad, love, wedding, anniversary, memorial…HeartPink
      work, job, client, report, office, contract, proposal…StarYellow

      3. Add reminders

      • Click ＋ Add reminder to create a reminder.
      • Type the reminder with a time, e.g. 9:00 go to eat.
      • Choose a level of emergency: Low, Medium, or High (higher = louder alarm).
      • Click 📝 to add remarks.
      • Click ✓ to collapse; click × to delete.

      When the reminder time arrives, a full-screen alarm pops up. Click Close Alarm to stop it and remove the reminder.

      4. Set your daily mood

      Click Mark your mood below the calendar and pick an emoji. The emoji appears on that day. For today, the picker only pops up once; click the emoji or the mood text to change it later.

      5. Holidays

      Click the Religious holidays toggle in the top-right to show or hide religious holidays.

      6. Events & Tasks

      Use the form above the calendar to add timed events or tasks. They appear on the calendar and in the Upcoming list. Complete tasks to keep your day marked as finished.

      7. Data

      Events, tasks, and study sessions are saved to your account. Occasions, reminders, and moods are saved in your browser's localStorage, so they stay after refresh but only on this browser/device.
